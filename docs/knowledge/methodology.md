# 薄肌 (Lean Muscle) Methodology

Aesthetic goal: Greek-God / lean-muscle. Prioritize visible upper-body detail over raw mass. Stay under ~15% body fat year-round.

## Specialization priorities (in order)

1. **Upper chest** — gives the "armour plate" appearance, fills the collar gap.
2. **Side delts** — widens the shoulder-to-waist ratio, the dominant cue for "athletic."
3. **Rear delts** — postural balance, prevents the "hunched lifter" silhouette, balances pressing volume.
4. **Back detail** (lats + mid-back) — V-taper depth, visible when lean.

Maintain (not specialize): biceps, triceps, core, calves. Train these 1× per week, just enough to retain.

De-prioritize for this user: heavy squats / deadliftsmax-outs (knee tendon load). Train legs for proportion and tendon health, not PRs.

## Weekly volume landmarks (effective sets per muscle per week)

`effective sets` = sets where `form_quality >= 3`; the `coach_trend` tool already discounts `form_quality < 3` by 50%.

| Muscle           | Maintenance | Specialization (MEV→MAV→MRV) |
|------------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| Upper chest      | 6           | 8 → 12 → 16                 |
| Side delt        | 6           | 8 → 14 → 20                 |
| Rear delt        | 8            | 10 → 16 → 20                |
| Lats              | 8            | 10 → 14 → 18                |
| Mid-back         | 8            | 10 → 14 → 18               |
| Biceps          | 6            | 8 → 12 → 16                 |
| Triceps         | 4            | 6 → 10 → 14                 |
| Quads            | 6            | 8 → 12 → 16                 |

MEV = Minimum Effective Volume (when detraining). MAV = Most Adaptive. MRV = Max Recoverable Volume — going past here stalls or regresses. Specialization block runs MAV; off-blocks run MEV.

## Specialization cycle structure

A 4-week specialization block targets ONE priority muscle (chest OR side delt OR back). Other specialization muscles drop to MEV. Run 3 blocks back-to-back cycling the three main priorities; one deload week between blocks if recovery drops below 70.

Read `progression.md` for RPE autoregulation and deload rules.
Read `tendinopathy.md` before prescribing any pressing or skull-crusher pattern.
