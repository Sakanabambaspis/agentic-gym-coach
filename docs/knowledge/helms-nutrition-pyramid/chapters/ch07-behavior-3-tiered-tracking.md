# Behavior & Lifestyle I: The 3-Tiered Tracking System

## Core Idea
Precision is a **dial, not a switch**: tracking strictness should match your situation (contest prep vs cut vs off-season), and missing a target doesn't mean falling off the diet — it means **dropping one tier down the precision scale and carrying on**. The 3-tier system replaces all-or-nothing tracking with a continuum, plus a graduation path to habit-based eating.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The wide path vs the tightrope**: off-season = wide path of dietary leeway; lean + restricted = an ever-narrowing path becoming a tightrope. Physiological reasons the lean dieter has less slack: lean bodies liberate more energy from body protein; caloric restriction drops testosterone in lean men (not the overweight); leanness predicts lean-mass loss while dieting; glycogen and intramuscular triglyceride depletion degrade training; bigger deficits cost strength and muscle retention; dieting is psychologically stressful. **Match precision to path width.**
- **The 3 tracking tiers + graduation (descending precision)**:

| Tier | Target | Tolerances |
|---|---|---|
| **Best** | hit all macros | contest prep **±5 g** · fat-loss **±10 g** · off-season **±20 g** P/C & **±10 g** fat |
| **Better** | hit protein + calories | protein as above; calories **±100 kcal** |
| **Good** | hit calories | **±100 kcal** (mainly off-season) |
| **Habit-based** | habits + weekly weight avg | after calibration (below) |

- **Usage doctrine**: aim Best when tracking isn't stressful, and treat Better/Good as regular, acceptable outcomes — social events, restaurants, holidays, alcohol, or when hitting protein would blow the calorie cap. A missed top tier is a planned event, not a failure.
- **Borrowing**: move up to **20% of a day's calories/macros to another day** (planned events, hunger swings); or track a **7-day average** instead of daily targets (2,500/day = any distribution averaging 2,500). Take the 24-h period off its pedestal — except in deep contest prep, where daily adherence *is* the plan (hunger constant, satiety gone, the plan the only tool).
- **Relearning hunger/satiety — the one-day estimation test**: after months of numbers-driven eating, eat freely by feel for one day; jot foods and rough portions (no macros); estimate the day's calories/macros from memory; compare. Close estimate + no wild over/undereating = ready for a more intuitive approach.
- **Habit-based tracking (graduated)**: off-season appropriate, run on identified habit gaps + 7-day bodyweight averages:
  - Need to gain but losing/maintaining → be **a little full at most meals**; verify by scale.
  - Gaining too fast → smaller portions, **stop just when satisfied**, eat slower.
  - Don't stress the carb/fat split off-season (20% vs 40% fat days are noise) — **calories + protein are the levers**.
  - Protein 0.8–1.0 g/lb (higher while dieting); habitually low → add 1–2 shakes; above range harmless unless it crowds fat/carbs.
  - ≥1 fruit + 1 vegetable serving per 1,000 kcal (Level 3 habits); visible fruit, one salad/day ≈ covers veg and fiber.
- **Systems stack**: tiers, borrowing, and habits are non-exclusive — e.g. run "Better" on an event day *and* borrow 20% to tomorrow; or live habit-based and periodically re-verify with tiered tracking.

## Anti-patterns
- **Binary tracking** ("hit macros or the day is ruined") — the exact mindset the tiers dissolve.
- The **two-rigid-options trap**: macro-perfection-for-life vs meal-plan-for-life — neither sustainable, both on/off mindset factories.
- **Contest-prep precision in an off-season** — stress without benefit.
- **Trusting hunger while very lean/dieting** — at that depth only the plan protects you.
- **Habit-based eating before passing the estimation test.**

## Worked Example
**Borrowing around a social event**: target 2,500 kcal/day, dinner out Saturday → take 20% (500 kcal) off Friday and add to Saturday; or simply hold the 7-day average at 2,500 and let 3,000/2,000/2,700/2,300/2,500×3 average out. Physiologically free in a non-depleted body.

**Estimation test scoring**: you jot "oats, whey, chicken-rice-broccoli ×2, pizza slice ×2, apple" and mentally call it ~2,900 kcal, 160 P; the log reconstructs 3,050 kcal, 150 P — close, no binge → cleared for habit-based eating, with bodyweight averages as the guardrail.

## Key Takeaways
1. Precision by situation: ±5 g prep · ±10 g cut · ±20 g / habits off-season; calories ±100 kcal as the fallback band.
2. Miss a target → drop a tier and continue; never binary.
3. Borrow ≤20% between days or run weekly averages (not in deep prep).
4. Graduate to habits via the estimation test; guard with 7-day weight averages.
5. Off-season essentials: calories, protein 0.8–1.0 g/lb, produce per 1,000 kcal.
