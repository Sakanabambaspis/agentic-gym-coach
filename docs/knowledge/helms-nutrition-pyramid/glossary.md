# Glossary

**Accuracy-flexibility-consistency triangle** — The mindset framework: precision costs flexibility, and only consistency actually produces results; required accuracy scales with the goal (Ch 1)

**Activity multiplier** — 1.3–2.2 factor (sedentary→very active, +3–6 lifting days) applied to bodyweight ×10 to estimate maintenance (Ch 2)

**Adaptive thermogenesis / metabolic adaptation** — TDEE falls ~18% below prediction (8–28% spread) after ≥10% weight loss; partially reversed by diet breaks (Ch 5)

**All-or-nothing thinking** — Binary on/off dieting mindset; the root of rigid meal plans, "magic" macros, and good/bad food lists (Ch 1)

**BCAAs** — Branched-chain amino acids; useful only when glycogen-depleted/low-carb/fast; redundant on adequate protein (Ch 6)

**Borrowing** — Moving up to 20% of a day's calories/macros to another day, or hitting weekly-average targets (Ch 7)

**Beta-alanine** — Muscle-endurance buffer; only for continuous efforts ≥60 s (Ch 6)

**CreaPure** — Licensed high-grade creatine monohydrate raw supplier; a quality signal (Ch 6)

**Creatine monohydrate** — 0.018 g/lb/day; saturates ~2 weeks; timing/loading irrelevant; the most proven supplement (Ch 6)

**D-aspartic acid** — Cautionary tale: 2009 T-spike → null → later evidence of lowered T in trained men (Ch 6)

**Diet break** — Planned 1–3-week return to ~maintenance (+300–600 kcal, cardio halved) for diets ≥3 months (Ch 5)

**Energy balance (Level 1)** — Calorie intake vs expenditure; target rates: lose 0.5–1.0%/week, gain 0.5–1.5%/month by training age (Ch 2)

**Error-margin principle** — Order restaurant foods whose worst-case estimation error is small; hidden oil ≈250 kcal per 2 tbsp (Ch 8)

**Examine.com / JISSN** — The two go-to resources for supplement validity/effectiveness (Ch 6)

**Fish oil (EPA+DHA)** — 2–3 g/day combined; leptin signaling, inflammation, mood, joints (Ch 6)

**Fiber** — 10 g per 1,000 kcal (min 20 g women / 25 g men), max 20% of carb intake; counted as carbs (Ch 3)

**Flexible dietary restraint** — The research-backed style associated with keeping lost weight off while staying sane (Ch 1)

**Framily** — Friends + family; briefed at the journey's start on what/why/importance/ask (Ch 8)

**Glycogen** — Stored carbohydrate; resynthesis completes within ~24 h; basis for refeed placement (Ch 5)

**Habit-based tracking** — Numbers-free tier after the estimation test: calories, protein, produce habits + 7-day weight averages (Ch 7)

**HMB** — Leucine metabolite; 3 g pre-training while cutting if money to burn; weak evidence in trained populations (Ch 6)

**Hydration checks** — 2/3 × bodyweight (lb) in fl oz (1 L/23 kg); calibrate to 5 clear urinations/day, 2 post-training; 2–3% dehydration costs performance (Ch 4)

**Inclusive vs exclusive mindset** — Include micronutrient-dense variety first; treats fit after — never villainize foods (Ch 1, Ch 4)

**Insulin resistance branch** — Higher-fat (35%+), lower-carb (0.5–1.5 g/lb) diets for ~20% of people; predictors: age, family diabetes history, PCOS, oligomenorrhea (Ch 3)

**Interference effect (cardio)** — Moderate-intensity cardio dose-dependently interferes with strength/hypertrophy; rules: cardio ≤ half of lifting time, ≤2 HIIT ≤30 min, ≤1 h moderate (Ch 2)

**Leucine check** — Whey quality flag: leucine <2.7 g per 25 g protein = possible spiking (Ch 6)

**Macronutrient prescription (mixed method)** — Protein g/lb, fat %, carbs remainder: cut 1.1–1.3 g/lb + 15–25% fat; gain 0.8–1.0 g/lb + 20–30% fat (Ch 3)

**Maintenance calories** — Intake producing no weight change; found by 2-week tracking (preferred) or bodyweight×10×multiplier (Ch 2)

**Meal frequency** — 3–6 meals/day for most; energy expenditure is frequency-neutral; pattern consistency matters (Ch 5)

**Metabolic magic** — Tissue-energy accounting: muscle ≈800–830 kcal/lb vs pure fat 4,270 — weight can rise in a deficit (recomp) or fat can rise in a deficit (muscle loss) (Ch 2)

**Multivitamin** — One normal-dosed daily while cutting as a micronutrient safety net; no mega-dose packs (Ch 6)

**NEAT** — Non-exercise activity thermogenesis; subconscious movement regulating expenditure; why individuals at the same size need different calories (Ch 2)

**Nutrition Pyramid** — Energy balance → macros → micros/water → timing → supplements, wrapped in Behavior & Lifestyle (Ch 1)

**Oligomenorrhea** — Menstrual cycles >35 days; insulin-resistance predictor; common in strength-sport women (Ch 3)

**One-day estimation test** — Eat freely, log foods only, estimate kcal/macros from memory, compare; pass = ready for habit-based eating (Ch 7)

**PCOS** — Polycystic ovarian syndrome; androgen-driven insulin resistance; higher-fat diets evidence-supported (Ch 3)

**Peri-workout protein** — 0.18–0.23 g/lb 1–2 h pre and post training; daily total dominates (Ch 5)

**Proprietary blend** — Ingredient list without doses; standard under-dosing disguise — avoid (Ch 6)

**Protein spiking** — Padding protein tests with cheap glycine/taurine; detected via price, labels, leucine content (Ch 6)

**Rate of weight gain** — Beginner 1–1.5%/month, intermediate 0.5–1%, advanced ≤0.5% (surplus ≈100–300 kcal/day) (Ch 2)

**Reactive hypoglycemia** — Blood-sugar dip from carbs too close to training; mitigate with fat in the pre-workout meal (Ch 5)

**Refeeds** — Maintenance-calorie days driven by carbs: 1/week early in a diet → 2 consecutive or 3 spread days below ~12% (M)/20% (F) BF (Ch 5)

**3500-calorie rule** — 1 lb adipose ≈ 3,500 kcal; useful simplification for converting weight trends to calorie adjustments (Ch 2)

**Small-social-footprint principle** — You chose to diet; arrange prep to minimally affect loved ones (DD, date night in) (Ch 8)

**Tracking tiers (Good/Better/Best)** — Precision continuum: Best ±5/10/20 g macros by phase; Better protein+±100 kcal; Good ±100 kcal; miss = drop a tier (Ch 7)

**Training wheels (meal plans)** — Example plans used briefly to learn food-to-target mapping, then abandoned (Ch 1)

**Unsolicited advice** — Never give it; pull-not-push advice etiquette (Ch 8)

**USP / NSF / GMP** — Third-party quality certifications for supplements (Ch 6)

**Vitamin D3** — 9–36 IU/lb/day by sun exposure; immune, mood, performance relevance (Ch 6)

**Water-soluble vs fat-soluble vitamins** — B+C (daily replenishment, hard to overdose) vs ADEK (stored, slow deficiency, overdose risk) (Ch 4)

**Weigh-in protocol** — Daily, morning, post-bathroom, pre-food, nude; act on 7-day averages only; 1–2% daily fluctuation is water (Ch 1)

**Wide path vs tightrope** — Off-season leeway vs contest-prep precision; match tracking tier to path width (Ch 7)

**40/20 self-trial** — Alternate months at 40% and 20% fat (same calories/protein), rating mood/energy/gym quality 1–10 to find your fat-carb ratio (Ch 3)
