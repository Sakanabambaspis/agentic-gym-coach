## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 1. The Nutrition Pyramid — order of importance
**Energy balance → Macronutrients → Micronutrients/water → Nutrient timing → Supplements**, wrapped in **Behavior & Lifestyle**. Level 1 is "70–80% of the battle"; supplements mostly "burn a hole in your wallet." Judge foods by *fit within the day's numbers* — "no good or bad foods, only good or bad diets." Mindset gate: **balance accuracy, flexibility, consistency** — flexible (not rigid) dietary restraint is what predicts weight loss kept off; all-or-nothing thinking (rigid meal plans, "magic" macros, good/bad food lists) manufactures binges. Meal plans and macro targets are training wheels, not commandments.

### 2. Energy balance (Level 1)
- **Maintenance**: track food + daily weigh-ins for 2 weeks → maintenance = avg intake − (weekly Δweight × 3500 ÷ 7). Fallback equation: BW(lb) × 10 × activity multiplier (1.3–1.6 sedentary → 1.9–2.2 very active, all including 3–6 lifting days).
- **Cut at 0.5–1.0% bodyweight/week** (deficit 500–1,000 kcal/day). Faster rates cost muscle regardless of protein.
- **Gain by training age**: beginner 1–1.5%/month (+200–300 kcal/day), intermediate 0.5–1% (+100–200), advanced ≤0.5% (≤100 — chase gym progress, not scale weight; small surplus gained the same muscle as +600 kcal with one-fifth the fat).
- **NEAT** makes every calorie number a hypothesis — adjust by weekly weight averages. **Cardio**: diet drives the deficit; cardio ≤ half of lifting time, ≤2 HIIT ≤30 min, ≤1 h moderate (worst interference), rest low-intensity/low-impact.
- **Metabolic magic**: muscle ≈800–830 kcal/lb vs pure fat ≈4,270 — scale weight can rise in a deficit (recomp, diet-break glycogen) or fall in a surplus; trust trends, not single readings.

### 3. Macronutrients (Level 2) — protein by bodyweight, fat by %, carbs take the rest
| Phase | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cutting | **1.1–1.3 g/lb** (2.3–2.8 g/kg) | **15–25%** | remainder |
| Gaining | **0.8–1.0 g/lb** (1.8–2.2 g/kg) | **20–30%** | remainder |

Floors: **fat 0.25 g/lb, carbs 0.5 g/lb**. kcal/g: protein 4, carb 4, fat 9. Worked 200 lb male: gaining @3,200 → 200 P / 90 F / 400 C; cutting @2,500 → 225 P / 55 F / 275 C. **Fiber**: 10 g per 1,000 kcal (min 20 g F / 25 g M), max 20% of carbs. ~20% of people do better higher-fat (insulin resistance signs: age, family diabetes history, PCOS, oligomenorrhea) — verify with the 40%/20%-fat alternating-month self-trial rating mood/energy/gym quality.

### 4. Micronutrients & water (Level 3)
Dieting shrinks the micronutrient budget — classic bodybuilder deficiencies: **vitamin D, calcium, zinc, magnesium, iron** (zinc ↓ = −194 to −527 kcal RMR; iron ↓ = strength loss). Defense: keep dairy + lean red meat + direct sun while cutting; **1 fruit + 1 vegetable serving per 1,000 kcal/day** (round up cutting; floor 2+2 gaining). **Fluids**: 2/3 × BW(lb) in fl oz (1 L/23 kg), everything except alcohol counts, then calibrate to **5 clear urinations/day, 2 post-training** — 2% dehydration hits aerobic performance, 3% hits strength.

### 5. Nutrient timing (Level 4) — scale order: diet → week → day → workout
- **Diet breaks**: dieting ≥3 months → 1–3 weeks at ~maintenance (+300–600 kcal, cardio ÷2) every ~8 weeks; reverses part of metabolic adaptation (TDEE −18% average after ≥10% loss), restores glycogen/sanity.
- **Refeeds**: 1 maintenance-carb day/week from the start (deepen the other 6 days: 750→875 kcal/day for a 1.5 lb/week target); below **~12% (M) / ~20% (F) BF** → 2 consecutive or 3 spread maintenance days. Place the day before high-volume sessions (AM/PM training) or same day (evening).
- **Meal frequency**: 3–6 meals, consistent pattern; frequency itself is neutral (2–7 identical for expenditure); extremes hurt adherence/hunger.
- **Peri-workout**: daily protein total dominates; pragmatic — **0.18–0.23 g/lb protein 1–2 h pre and post**; while cutting add 10–20% of daily carbs pre-workout; post-workout carb "windows" mostly don't matter for lifters (glycogen resynthesis ≤24 h; protein spikes insulin fine); ≥2 h continuous hard training → 8–15 g protein + 30–60 g carbs liquid.

### 6. Supplements (Level 5) — filters first, short list second
**Filters**: quality (third-party lab analysis, USP/NSF/GMP stamps, named suppliers like Creapure, *never* proprietary blends) → validity/effectiveness (Examine.com, JISSN) → replication before novelty (D-aspartic acid: hyped → null → counterproductive). **The list**: multivitamin (cutting only, normal doses) · EPA+DHA 2–3 g/day · vitamin D3 9–36 IU/lb/day by sun · **creatine monohydrate 0.018 g/lb/day, timing irrelevant** · caffeine (tiredness 0.45–1.40 mg/lb/day; performance 1.81–2.72 mg/lb ≤2×/week, start at half) · beta-alanine 3–4 g only for 60 s+ efforts · BCAA only when depleted/low-carb/fast · HMB barely, cutting only. Whey fraud check: market-rate price + leucine ≥2.7 g per 25 g protein.

### 7. Behavior & Lifestyle — the adherence wrapper
- **3-tier tracking**: Best = macros (prep ±5 g · cut ±10 g · off-season ±20 g P/C & ±10 g fat) · Better = protein + ±100 kcal · Good = ±100 kcal · Habit-based after passing the one-day estimation test. **Missing a target = drop a tier and carry on** — never binary.
- **Borrowing**: ≤20% of a day's calories shifted to another day, or 7-day-average targets (not in deep prep, where the plan is the only tool — hunger lies when very lean).
- **Eating out**: order for bounded worst-case error (sirloin + plain broccoli vs pasta — hidden oil ≈250 kcal); prep ~monthly for can't-miss events, cuts 1–2×/wk, off-season 1–2×+/wk. 28 meals/week perspective.
- **Alcohol**: 7 kcal/g, no fourth macro → automatic tier drop; ≤15% of daily calories, no next-day effect, ≤2×/week.
- **Social**: brief "framily" at the start (what/why/importance/when/ask); small footprint (be the DD, date night in); **never unsolicited advice** — when asked, what + logical why, zero bashing.
