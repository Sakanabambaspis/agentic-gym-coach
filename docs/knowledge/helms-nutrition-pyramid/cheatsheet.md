# Cheatsheet — Decision Rules & Thresholds

## Pyramid Order (fix L1 before debating L5)
**Energy balance → Macros → Micros/water → Timing → Supplements**, wrapped in Behavior & Lifestyle. "No good or bad foods, only good or bad diets."

## Level 1 — Calories
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Maintenance (best) | 2-week track: avg intake − (weekly Δlb × 3500 ÷ 7) |
| Maintenance (fast) | BW lb ×10 (kg ×22) × 1.3–1.6/1.5–1.8/1.7–2.0/1.9–2.2 |
| Cut rate | **0.5–1.0% BW/week** (−500 to −1,000 kcal/day) |
| Gain rate | Beginner 1–1.5%/mo (+200–300 kcal) · Intermediate 0.5–1% (+100–200) · Advanced ≤0.5% (≤100, judge by gym progress) |
| Cardio caps | ≤ half of lifting time; ≤2 HIIT ≤30 min; ≤1 h moderate; rest low-intensity; low-impact |
| Cardio burn | light 0.2 / moderate 0.45 / vigorous 0.7 kcal per 10 min per lb |

**Adjust by weekly averages only** — NEAT/adaptation make every number a hypothesis.

## Level 2 — Macros
| Phase | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cutting | **1.1–1.3 g/lb** | **15–25%** | remainder |
| Gaining | **0.8–1.0 g/lb** | **20–30%** | remainder |

Floors: fat 0.25 g/lb · carbs 0.5 g/lb. Fiber: 10 g/1,000 kcal (min 20 F / 25 M), max 20% of carbs, count as carbs. kcal/g: P4 C4 F9 (alcohol 7).
Insulin-resistance signs (age, family DM, PCOS, oligomenorrhea) → try higher-fat branch; verify with the 40%/20% fat month-trial (rate mood/energy/gym quality 1–10 daily).

## Level 3 — Micros & Water
- Dieting-deficiency five: **vit D, calcium, zinc, magnesium, iron** → keep dairy + lean red meat + real sun while cutting.
- Produce: **1 fruit + 1 veg serving per 1,000 kcal** (round up cutting; floor 2+2 gaining).
- Fluids: **2/3 × BW(lb) in fl oz** (1 L/23 kg) → calibrate to **5 clear urinations/day (2 post-training)**. All drinks count except alcohol. 2% dehydration costs aerobic, 3% costs strength.

## Level 4 — Timing
| Tool | Trigger | Prescription |
|---|---|---|
| Diet break | dieting ≥3 mo | 1–3 wk at maintenance (+300–600 kcal, cardio ÷2), ~every 8 wk |
| 1-day refeed | from diet start | 1×/wk maintenance via carbs; deepen other 6 days |
| Multi-day refeeds | <12% M / <20% F BF | 2 consecutive or 3 spread maintenance-carb days |
| Meal frequency | always | 3–6/day, consistent pattern; frequency itself neutral |
| Peri-workout | cutting matters most | 0.18–0.23 g/lb protein + 10–20% daily carbs, 1–2 h pre AND post; ≥2 h continuous training → 8–15 g P + 30–60 g C liquid |

Refeed placement: day before high-volume sessions (AM/PM trainers); same day only for evening trainers.

## Level 5 — Supplements
| Supplement | Dose |
|---|---|
| Multivitamin | 1 normal-dosed, cutting only |
| EPA+DHA | 2–3 g/day combined |
| Vitamin D3 | 9–36 IU/lb/day by sun |
| Creatine mono | 0.018 g/lb/day, timing irrelevant |
| Caffeine | tiredness 0.45–1.40 mg/lb/day · performance 1.81–2.72 mg/lb ≤2×/wk, start half-dose |
| Beta-alanine | 3–4 g/day only for 60 s+ efforts |
| BCAA | 0.09 g/lb pre only if depleted/low-carb/fasted |
| HMB | 3 g pre, cutting + spare cash only |

**Buy filters**: 3rd-party lab or USP/NSF/GMP · no proprietary blends · leucine ≥2.7 g/25 g protein · wait for replication on new stuff (D-AA lesson). Verify at Examine.com / JISSN.

## Behavior — Tracking Tiers
| Tier | Target | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Best | macros | prep ±5 g · cut ±10 g · off-season ±20 P/C, ±10 F |
| Better | protein + kcal | ±100 kcal |
| Good | kcal | ±100 kcal |
| Habit-based | habits + 7-day weight avg | after estimation test |

**Miss = drop a tier and continue.** Borrow ≤20% between days. Weigh daily (morning, post-bathroom, nude), act on weekly averages; 1–2% daily swings are water.

## Behavior — Out & Alcohol
- Eating out: prep ~monthly (sirloin + double broccoli, no butter); cut 1–2×/wk conservative; off-season 1–2×+/wk.
- Worst-case-error ordering: hidden oil ≈ **250 kcal** you can't taste.
- Alcohol: 7 kcal/g → auto Better/Good tier; ≤**15%** daily kcal; no next-day effect; ≤2×/wk.
- 28 meals/week perspective: 1–2 imperfect = noise.

## Social Rules
Brief framily at the start (what/why/importance/when/ask). Small footprint (DD, cook date night in). **No unsolicited advice**; when asked: what + logical why, zero bashing.

## Start-Tight Rule
New to tracking → err on precision; flexibility is earned as food knowledge grows. Meal plans = training wheels only.
