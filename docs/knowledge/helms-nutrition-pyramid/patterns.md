# Patterns & Techniques

## Setup Patterns

**Maintenance by 2-week tracking (preferred)**
**When to use**: whenever time allows; not valid for novices or post-layoff lifters.
**How**: track calories + daily weigh-ins for 14 days; compare week-1 vs week-2 weight averages; maintenance = average intake − (weekly weight change × 3500 ÷ 7).
**Trade-offs**: most accurate, individualized; costs two weeks of discipline.

**Maintenance by equation (fallback)**
BW(lb) × 10 (kg × 22) × activity multiplier (1.3–1.6 sedentary → 1.9–2.2 very active, all +3–6 lifting days). Use the middle of the range; expect large error.

**Macro setup (mixed method)**
Protein by bodyweight → fat by percentage → carbs take the remainder. Cut: 1.1–1.3 g/lb + 15–25% fat. Gain: 0.8–1.0 g/lb + 20–30% fat. Floors: fat 0.25 g/lb, carbs 0.5 g/lb.

**Rate-setting by goal**
Cut 0.5–1.0% BW/week (deficit ≈ 500–1,000 kcal/day). Gain by training age: beginner 1–1.5%/month (+200–300 kcal/day), intermediate 0.5–1% (+100–200), advanced ≤0.5% (+≤100, judge by gym progress not scale).

## Cardio Patterns

**Deficit split** — diet creates most of the deficit; cardio capped: total ≤ half of lifting time; ≤2 HIIT × ≤30 min; ≤1 h moderate; rest low-intensity; low-impact modalities (cycle, row, swim, elliptical).

**Cardio calorie math** — ~0.2/0.45/0.7 kcal per 10 min per lb BW above rest for light/moderate/vigorous.

## Diet-Phase Management Patterns

**Diet break** — diets ≥3 months: 1–3 weeks at ~maintenance (+300–600 kcal, cardio ÷2); schedule every ~8 weeks (e.g. weeks 8/16/24 of a prep).

**Single-day refeed** — 1 day/week at maintenance via carbs (protein/fat unchanged); deepen the other 6 days to hold the weekly deficit (e.g. 750→875 kcal/day).

**Multi-day refeeds (lean)** — below ~12% M / ~20% F BF: 2 consecutive or 3 spread maintenance-carb days/week; place the day before high-volume sessions (morning/afternoon trainers) or same day (evening trainers).

**Weekend flex (gaining)** — weekdays no lower than 10% below maintenance; banked calories fund weekend headroom.

## Tracking Patterns

**3-Tier precision matching** — aim Best (macros ±5/10/20 g by prep/cut/off-season); accept Better (protein + ±100 kcal) and Good (±100 kcal) as planned fallbacks; miss = drop a tier, never "off the diet."

**Borrowing** — ≤20% of a day's calories moved to another day; or 7-day-average targets. Not in deep prep.

**One-day estimation test** — free-eating day + food log only → memory estimate vs actual; pass = habit-based ready.

**Habit-based tier** — off-season: 7-day weight averages + habit fixes (a little full to gain; stop-at-satisfied + slower eating if too fast; protein 0.8–1.0 g/lb; produce per 1,000 kcal).

**Weigh-in protocol** — daily, standardized conditions; act only on 7-day averages (14-day if noisy).

## Eating-Out & Alcohol Patterns

**Error-margin ordering** — bounded-worst-case meals only in a cut (lean steak + double broccoli, no butter); never trust listed macros on energy-dense dishes; hidden oil ≈250 kcal/2 tbsp.

**Frequency caps** — prep ~monthly (important events); cut 1–2×/wk conservative; off-season 1–2×+/wk with tier fallbacks.

**Alcohol pattern** — automatic Better/Good tier; protein held, alcohol debited from calories; ≤15% of daily kcal; no next-day effect; ≤2×/wk.

**Perspective math** — ~28 meals/week; 1–2 imperfect ones are noise if protein + weight trend hold.

## Supplement Patterns

**Three-filter purchase** — quality (3rd-party lab / USP-NSF-GMP / named raw supplier; no proprietary blends) → validity (Examine.com Human Effect Matrix) → effectiveness (relevant, significant).

**Whey fraud check** — market-rate price; no proprietary blend; leucine ≥2.7 g per 25 g protein.

**New-supplement ladder** — no evidence: ignore · surrogate endpoints: watch · one direct study: wait for independent replication (D-AA lesson).

**Core stack** — creatine 0.018 g/lb/day (any time); EPA+DHA 2–3 g; D3 9–36 IU/lb by sun; multi while cutting; caffeine split-dosed by purpose (tiredness 0.45–1.40 mg/lb daily; performance 1.81–2.72 mg/lb ≤2×/wk).

## Social Patterns

**Framily script** — at the start: what → why → importance → when hardest → explicit support ask → invite reciprocal feedback.

**Small footprint** — keep attending in modified form (DD + diet soda; date night in; movie dates).

**Advice etiquette** — pull not push; what + logical why; zero bashing; point to resources.

## Mindset Patterns

**Inclusive-first eating** — micronutrient-dense variety in first, treats after; no food moralizing.

**Meal plans as training wheels** — use example plans (or EatThisMuch) briefly to learn targets, then diverge.

**Stop-and-resume** — blew a target? Stop there; next day resumes. The pizza response to a 5 g overshoot is the error, not the overshoot.
